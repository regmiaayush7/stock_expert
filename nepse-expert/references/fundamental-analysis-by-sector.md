# Fundamental Analysis by Sector (nepse-expert v1 playbook)

## Commercial & Development Banks
- **Core metrics**: Net Interest Margin (NIM), Spread, Base Rate, Credit-Deposit (CD) ratio, Non-Performing Loans (NPL), Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR), Cost of Funds, Loan-Loss Provisioning, Return on Equity (ROE), Return on Assets (ROA), Book Value per Share (BVPS), EPS, PE, PBV, Distributable Profit (after NRB regulatory reserves), Dividend Capacity, Bonus vs. Cash mix, Paid-up capital rules, Merger pressure.
- **Formulas**:
  - *Distributable Profit*: Net Profit after tax minus Regulatory Reserves (General Reserve, Actuarial Gain/Loss, Corporate Social Responsibility, Staff Training, Regulatory Adjustments).
  - *NIM*: Net Interest Income / Average Earning Assets.

## Hydropower
- **Core metrics**: Installed Capacity (MW), Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) rate & escalation (Wet vs. Dry season rates), Commercial Operation Date (COD), Wet vs. Dry generation ratio, Spill risk, Debt-to-Equity & Interest burden, Project stage (Construction vs. Operating), River basin & hydrological risk (flood/landslide), Tariff & NEA payment delays, Export potential (India/Bangladesh), EPS volatility.
- **Valuation Rule**: Cost per MW and DCF on PPA cash flows, adjusted for escalation and dry-season generation drops.

## Life & Non-Life Insurance
- **Core metrics**: Premium growth, Claims ratio, Combined ratio, Solvency margin, Investment income, Bonus liability, Paid-up capital requirements, Merger incentives.

## Microfinance & Finance Companies
- **Core metrics**: Portfolio at Risk (PAR > 30 days), Net Interest Margin, Deprived-sector lending compliance, Interest rate caps compliance, Delinquency ratio, Capital needs.

## Manufacturing, Hotels, Trading & Investment Companies
- **Core metrics**: Capacity utilization, Operating margins, Demand drivers, Debt burden, Promoter holding, Look-through NAV for investment holding companies.

## Mutual Funds
- **Core metrics**: NAV vs. Market Price, Discount/Premium to NAV, Maturity and lock-in period, Distribution record, Portfolio asset allocation (Equity vs. Fixed Income).
