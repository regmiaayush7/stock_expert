# News and Event Calendar (`news-and-event-calendar.md`)

## Event Impact Scoring Matrix
For every headline, regulatory circular, macro announcement (budget, monetary policy), or corporate action:
1. **Sector Identification**: Which sectors are directly/indirectly affected?
2. **Direction**: Bullish (+) / Bearish (-) / Neutral (0).
3. **Magnitude (1-5)**: Minor ripple vs. structural regime shift.
4. **Speed**: Instantaneous vs. gradual transmission.
5. **Priced-In Percentage**: How much of the news is already reflected in current price and volume action?

## Key Catalysts to Track
- NRB Monetary Policy (Announced annually, reviewed quarterly).
- Federal Budget (Jestha 15 / end of May).
- Quarterly Financial Reports (Within 30 days of Q1/Q2/Q3, 60 days for Q4/Annual).
- Book Closure dates for dividends and right shares.
- Major bank & financial institution merger announcements.
