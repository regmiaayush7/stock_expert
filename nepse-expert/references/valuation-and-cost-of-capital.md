# Valuation and Cost of Capital (`valuation-and-cost-of-capital.md`)

## Cost of Equity for Nepal ($K_e$)
$$K_e = R_f + \beta \times ERP + \text{Illiquidity Premium} + \text{Country Risk}$$
- $R_f$: Yield on 1-year to 5-year Government Treasury Bonds / Development Bonds.
- $ERP$: Equity Risk Premium estimated for Nepal (historical average 6-8%).
- $\beta$: Stock beta relative to NEPSE Index.

## Valuation Methods
1. **Dividend Discount Model (DDM) & Residual Income**: For stable commercial banks and dividend-paying insurers.
2. **PBV-ROE Regression**: Cross-sectional regression across sector peers to identify undervalued institutions.
3. **Hydropower DCF**: PPA-based cash flow projection over concession period (typically 30 years) with wet/dry seasonal adjustments and debt amortization.
4. **Sum-of-the-Parts (SOTP) / NAV Discount**: For investment companies holding listed and unlisted shares.
5. **Reverse DCF**: Determining what growth rate today's market price implies.
6. **Margin of Safety**: Required discount to intrinsic value before a Buy/Accumulate verdict is issued.
