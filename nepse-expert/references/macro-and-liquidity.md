# Macro and Liquidity (`macro-and-liquidity.md`)

## Core Transmission Channels
- **Nepal Rastra Bank Policy Rate & Interest Rate Corridor**: Bank rate, repo rate, deposit rate. Determines cost of funds and commercial lending/deposit rates.
- **Credit Growth & CD Ratio**: Credit-Deposit ratio limits (typically capped at 85-90%) dictate bank lending capacity and market liquidity.
- **Remittance Inflows & Foreign Exchange Reserves**: Remittances drive domestic deposit growth, real estate demand, and share market liquidity.
- **NPR-Peg to INR**: Peg stability anchors imported inflation and monetary policy alignment with India.
- **Government Capital Spending**: Execution of budget capital expenditure in the second half of the fiscal year releases liquidity into the banking system.
