# Accounting, Governance, Behaviour & Portfolio Risk (`accounting-and-governance.md`)

## Accounting & Regulation (NFRS / NRB Directives)
- **Distributable Profit**: Strictly calculated after deducting regulatory reserves, actuarial gains, and statutory reserves from net profit.
- **Interest Income Recognition**: Interest accrued on non-performing loans cannot be recognized as income; must be kept in interest suspense.
- **Single-Obligor Limits & Margin Lending Caps**: NRB limits on bank exposure to single borrowers and stock market margin loans.

## Governance Red Flags
- Related-party transactions, promoter borrowing from own institution, board churn, dominant family control, repeated equity dilution, high promoter share pledging.

## Behaviour & Manipulation
- Retail herding, operator syndicates, circular trading in floorsheet, broker concentration, low-float squeezes, margin-call spirals, Telegram/social media rumor cycles.

## Portfolio Construction & Risk
- Position sizing limits, core/satellite split, cash buffer, stop-loss and thesis invalidation rules, tax-efficient holding periods (>365 days), and liquidity exit planning.
