**If the cut is real, how it hits**
- Banks: cheaper deposits help margins over time. But loans reprice down too, so near-term margin can shrink. The real gains are credit growth and fewer bad loans. Not an automatic buy.
- Hydro: most projects carry heavy debt, so lower interest cost lifts profit. But hydro earnings depend more on PPA rates, dry-season output and NEA payment delays. Rate relief is one factor of several.
- Headline pops fade fast. Buying at Monday's open means buying after the move.
