Apply if you like, but expect to get nothing. Applying for 1000 kitta does not raise your odds. Rs 300 is the ceiling on listing day, not a forecast.

**Is applying for the max a good idea?**

Recent hydro IPOs were oversubscribed 11x to 38x. In an oversubscribed issue, the lottery gives each lucky applicant 10 kitta, whether you applied for 10 or 1000. The extra Rs 99,000 you block in ASBA buys nothing in that case. Size matters only if the issue is undersubscribed. Then you get all 1000 kitta, which is Rs 1,00,000 in this company. So apply for 1000 only if you would be happy to hold that much. If not, apply for 10 and get the same odds.

Before you apply, read the prospectus. Check net worth per share, whether the plant is running or still being built, the power purchase agreement, debt, the promoters, and the rating. Also check the max kitta for the general public. I have not seen your company's prospectus. If you send me the name, I can check it.

**How likely is allotment?**

Your chance is roughly (public units / 10) / number of applicants. Recent examples:

- **Appolo Hydropower:** 7,80,200 public units and 27,27,117 valid applications. 78,020 people got 10 kitta each, about 2.9%.
- **Taksar Pikhuwa Khola:** about 25 lakh public units and 26.05 lakh applicants at closing, about 10%.

For 10 lakh units and 25 to 27 lakh applicants, expect about 2 to 4%. That is roughly 1 in 25 to 1 in 40. If the 10 lakh includes the quotas for locals, workers abroad and employees, the public part is smaller and your odds are lower. Each family member with their own demat and their own money is a separate ticket.

**Will it list at Rs 300?**

It might, but nothing guarantees it. NEPSE sets an opening range for the first trade. For most recent hydros it was Rs 100 to Rs 300. The range appears to scale with net worth per share. Beni's was 91.27 to 273.81 and Bhujung's was 90.04 to 270.12, each exactly 3x the lower end. So if your company's net worth per share is below Rs 100, the cap is below Rs 300 and Rs 300 is impossible.

Recent hydros have opened at or near the cap. Appolo's price never dropped below Rs 299.7 after day one and peaked at Rs 1,053 on 22 June. ShareHub showed about Rs 585 on 5 October, which may be stale. Beni opened at Rs 273, then reached Rs 313.90 on 15 October and Rs 353 on 16 October, on volumes of only 90 and 40 shares. These are aggregator numbers, so verify them on NEPSE. A hot run like this can end, and thin volume means you may not get out at the quoted price.

Listing also takes weeks after allotment. Appolo was allotted on 24 April and listed on 8 June. Expect your shares to sit for 1 to 2 months.

**Rough value of a win**

You get 10 kitta for Rs 1,000 and sell at Rs 300:

- Gross sale: Rs 3,000, so a gain of Rs 2,000.
- Capital gains tax at 5% (short-term rate): about Rs 100.
- Broker, SEBON and DP charges: about Rs 40.
- Net: about Rs 1,850.

At a 2 to 4% chance, that is worth about Rs 40 to Rs 75 per application. Treat it as a lottery ticket and not as an income plan.
