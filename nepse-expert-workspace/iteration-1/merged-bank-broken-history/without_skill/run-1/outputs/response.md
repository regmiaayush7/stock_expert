A 5-year chart drawn today (Oct 2026) starts around Oct 2021. Nothing before 2020 should appear on it. If you see 2019 or 2020, you are on a "max" or "all" range, or the chart is mislabelled. The bigger distortion in a 5-year chart is bonus shares, not the mergers. GBIME issued 10% (book closure Mangsir 13, 2078, about 28 Nov 2021), 3% (early 2023), 1% (Nov 2023), 5.5% (early 2025) and 4% (2026). One share bought in Oct 2021 is about 1.256 shares today. An unadjusted chart turns that into a fake extra loss.

**Can you trust the data?**

Partly.

- The merger facts and bonus percentages are solid enough, but they rest on Sharesansar reports. I did not cross-check against NEPSE or GBIME filings.
- The prices are not closes. Most are "as of writing" snapshots in news articles, so the start price could be off by a few percent, which moves the answer by several points.
- The 4% bonus: both sites show 396.4 million shares (39.64 arba), which is 38.11 arba plus 4%, so it looks listed. I did not see a listing notice.
- The sites are messy. Merolagani's GBIME dividend table repeats some fiscal years with different numbers and does not label cash versus bonus. Sharesansar's company page showed a "404" heading and a stale 2024 auction list.
- Test whether your chart is adjusted. Sharesansar's own notice adjusted GBIME from Rs 211 to Rs 208.91 on 28 Nov 2023 for the 1% bonus ([notice](https://www.sharesansar.com/eventdetail/price-adjusted-gbime-2023-11-28)). Look at the book closure around 28 Nov 2021. An adjusted chart shows no cliff there. An unadjusted one drops about 9% overnight.
- For a number you can act on, export an adjusted daily series from your broker or a charting site and use the exact 24 Oct 2021 close.
