**What to do with each stock**

1. **NHPC (54% of value, +29.85%): trim.**
   - Sell about 400 of the 1,000 units and hold the rest. That takes NHPC from 54% to about 32% of the portfolio.
   - It is already 24% below its 52-week high of 340, and one stock should not be more than half of everything.
   - Selling all 1,000 would net roughly Rs 52,800 to 56,500 after fees and capital gains tax (CGT).
   - Check your buy dates first. If you are a few weeks short of 365 days, waiting lowers the tax rate.
   - I could not find current EPS or dividend data for NHPC. This is a concentration call, not a valuation call.

2. **UPPER (16%, -22.8%): hold, do not average down.**
   - Your cost of Rs 250 is above its 52-week high of 242.60. Waiting to "get back to 250" is not a plan.
   - FY 82/83 quarterly profits were positive: Rs 1.67 bn, 0.997 bn and 0.287 bn. I found no dividend declared.
   - Decide when the Q1 FY 83/84 results come out, around mid-November. Last year they came on 16 Nov.
   - If you would not buy it fresh at 193, sell then.
   - Do not sell only to book a tax loss. Setting losses off against other share gains is only proposed.
   - To cut hydropower exposure, trim NHPC first.

3. **NICA (13%, -8.3%): hold, and plan cash for the right share.**
   - NICA plans a 2:1 right issue, which is 1 new share per 2 held, so 100 rights on your 200 shares. It has NRB preliminary approval and was in the SEBON pipeline on 28 July.
   - I found no price or book closure date. At par (Rs 100), subscribing would cost about Rs 10,000.
   - Do not sell before the book closure unless you are happy to give up the entitlement.
   - The price will drop on the ex-right date, to about 247 at par. That is mechanical, not a crash.
   - I could not find its FY 82/83 dividend.

4. **NABIL (11%, +9.4%): hold.**
   - The 42nd AGM approved 15.80% for FY 82/83 (about 9 Oct): 5% bonus plus 10.80% cash. Profit was Rs 7.91 bn.
   - If you held on the 30 Sep book closure, expect 5 bonus shares and about Rs 1,000 cash after 5% tax. Neither is in this screenshot.
   - Check MeroShare. After the bonus your quantity should be 105 and your WACC about 476.

5. **SHL (5%, +30%): hold. It is a small position.**
   - It is near its 52-week high of 559.80.
   - Last year it paid 15% bonus plus 16.58% cash, with book closure on 21 Nov. This year's dividend news should come soon.
   - To lock in the gain, selling all nets about Rs 5,300 to 5,650 after costs.
