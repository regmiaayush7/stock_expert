Not cheap. I would not buy 2000 kitta of SFCL at Rs 370. Three problems.

**1. Tomorrow morning the market is closed.**
Tomorrow is Sunday 25 Oct 2026. NEPSE now trades Monday to Friday, and it is also closed for the Dashain break through Kartik 8. Trading resumes Monday 26 Oct (Kartik 9) at 11:00 AM, not early morning. You cannot place the order tomorrow. **2. Rs 370 is probably a stale price, and the stock barely trades.**
I assume SFCL is Samriddhi Finance (formerly World Merchant Banking). Tell me if you meant another company.
- Merolagani and Sharesansar both show last traded price Rs 370.00 on **9 July 2026**. That is three and a half months ago. Merolagani shows 30-day average volume of 0.
- On that day only 5,290 shares traded. Your 2000 kitta is about 38% of that whole day's volume.
- On 9 June 2026 it traded 13,579 shares at Rs 385.1 and fell 3.24%. This is a thin stock.
- If you place a buy order, either it sits unfilled, or you push the price up by buying into thin sell orders. When you want to sell later, you hit the same thin market. Exit may take days or cost you a lower price.
- Check live market depth on TMS on Monday before you trust any price. Do not use "rs 370" from memory.

**3. Positive EPS does not make it cheap.**
- Latest EPS I could find is Rs 0.12, for Q3 of FY 2082/83. At Rs 370 that is a P/E in the thousands (Merolagani shows 3,083). Even if you annualise it, you pay about Rs 2,000+ for each rupee of yearly profit. Earnings are close to zero.
- Book value per share is Rs 60.30. At Rs 370 you pay about 6.1 times book value for a finance company. Paid-up value is Rs 100, so net worth per share is below par. That means accumulated losses.
- Older reports I found look bad. Q2 FY 2081/82: NPL jumped from 4.68% to 19.34%, EPS was negative Rs 15.34, reserves were negative about Rs 40.8 crore. Those are about 1.5 years old. I did not find the FY 2082/83 annual (Q4) report, so I cannot say if things improved. Check that first.
- No cash dividend record on the page. Bonus and right share were last in FY 2078/79.
- 52-week range is roughly Rs 359 to Rs 450 on Merolagani (Sharesansar says high Rs 565; the sources disagree). The stock sits near its 52-week low because the market does not trust the earnings, not because it is cheap.
- A small finance company with weak capital can face regulator pressure or a forced merger. That can help or hurt shareholders. It is a bet, not a value buy.

**Size of the bet.** 2000 x Rs 370 = Rs 7,40,000 plus broker, SEBON and DP charges. In a stock that may not trade for weeks, that is a large amount to be stuck with.

**What I would do instead.**
1. Wait for Monday. Open the live quote and the buy/sell depth for SFCL. Note the real last price and the date.
2. Read the latest quarterly report on NEPSE or Merolagani. Look at NPL, net profit for the full year, net worth, and capital adequacy.
3. If you still want it, start small (a few hundred kitta at most), use a limit order, and never chase the ask.
4. Decide your sell condition before you buy. Illiquid stocks are hard to exit.

This is not a sure loss. A merger or a turnaround could lift the price. But "EPS positive, finance company" is not a reason to call it cheap. On the numbers I found, it looks expensive.
