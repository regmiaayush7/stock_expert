Downside to the 52-week low is bigger than upside to the 52-week high. I lean sell and FD, unless you can sit through a 10% drop. Not licensed advice.

**Check first, it can cost you money.** The FY 2082/83 dividend is 5% bonus + 10.8% cash. Sharesansar lists book closure as 30 Sep 2026, and the AGM approved it around 8-9 Oct. If that date is right, selling now does not lose the dividend. You still get about Rs 10,010 net cash (10.8% minus 5% tax on the full 15.8%) and 50 bonus kitta (about Rs 27,350 at 547). Confirm in MeroShare that you are entitled. I found the date on one page only. If you are not entitled yet, do not sell before book closure.

**Why I lean sell**
- 547 sits in the upper half of the 485-581 range. P/E is about 20-22 and P/B about 2.3-2.4. EPS shows as 24.66 on one site and 27.01 on another.
- Dividend yield is about 2.3% to 2.9% before tax. FD pays about 4.3% after tax with no price risk.
- You made +9.4% on price plus Rs 11,875 net dividend (from Jan 2026) in 21 months. That is about 12%, or 6.6% a year. Good, but it was a good stretch.
- Tailwind for holding: the CGT cut and the government's capital market reform plan. That is a bet, not a number.

**Keep it if** you do not need the money for 2+ years, this is a small part of your savings, and you believe in NABIL's dividend growth. A middle path is to sell 500 kitta (about Rs 271,800 cash), FD that, and keep 500 plus the 50 bonus.
