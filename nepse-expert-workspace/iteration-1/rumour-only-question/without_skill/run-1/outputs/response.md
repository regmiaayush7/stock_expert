No. Don't put everything in XYZ today. Three separate problems.

**1. It is probably insider information, and you would be trading on it.**
A bank employee knowing an unannounced bonus is inside information. Nepal's Securities Act prohibits insider trading, and that includes trading on a tip, not only the person who leaked it. SEBON can look at who bought just before an announcement. Your friend could also lose his job over this. Don't act on it, and I'd ask him not to share more.

**2. Even if he is right, "100% bonus" is not a 100% gain.**
A bonus gives you more shares, not more value. After the book closure the price adjusts down. With a 100% bonus (1 free share for every 1 held), you hold twice the shares at roughly half the price. Your holding is worth about the same on that day. Whatever you gain comes from how the market reacts, and a rumour that reached you usually reached others too, so some of it may already be in the price.

**3. "Announce" is not "done", and "everything" is the real mistake.**
- A board decision is only a proposal. Bonus shares normally still need AGM approval, and the regulators' approvals (NRB for a bank, then SEBON). It can be reduced or delayed.
- A 100% bonus from a bank is unusual, which is a reason to doubt the tip.
- You have to hold the shares on the book closure date to get the bonus. That date comes weeks after the announcement, so buying today gives you no entitlement advantage over buying after the news is public.
- Putting your whole portfolio into one stock on one verbal tip means one wrong detail (amount, timing, approval, market reaction) can hurt you badly. That is true even for a tip that is correct.

**What I'd do instead**
- Wait for the official announcement on NEPSE and the bank's own notice.
- Then decide on facts: the price after the news, the bank's earnings, its capital position, and what the bonus does to the per-share numbers.
- If you want to size a position, keep it a small part of your portfolio, not all of it.
