**2. Even if the tip is true, the money case is weak.**
- A bonus does not create value. With a 100% bonus you get 2 kitta for every 1, and the price roughly halves at the ex-date. Example: 100 kitta at Rs 500 becomes 200 kitta at about Rs 250. Same Rs 50,000, before costs. Your WACC halves too, so a later sale has a bigger taxable gain per kitta.
- "Announce" is not "paid". Bank bonuses are a board proposal, subject to Nepal Rastra Bank approval and the AGM. Book closure comes later. Examples: [Sindhu Bikas Bank, "subject to NRB, AGM approval"](https://www.sharesansar.com/index.php/newsdetail/sindhu-bikas-bank-announces-11-bonus-shares-to-shareholders-subject-to-nrb-agm-approval); [Nabil's 5% bonus, passed at its AGM on 8 Oct 2026](https://ekantipur.com/market/2026/10/08/179146772059016887.html).
- 100% looks very large for a bank. The recent bank bonuses I found were 5% (Nabil) and 6% (Everest). That is a handful of examples, not a survey. It is still a reason to doubt the size, the timing, or whether it is a proposal or a final decision. A tip can be wrong on any of them.
- Price risk is two-sided. The daily limit is now 15% (as of 2026-10-24, verify). If the stock runs to the upper circuit you may never get filled, or you buy at the top after others heard the same rumour. If it falls, a lower-circuit lock can trap you for several sessions.
