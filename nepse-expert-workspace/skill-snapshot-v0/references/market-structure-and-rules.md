## From memory, verify live before relying on them

- Face value Rs 100; the unit is the kitta; minimum IPO application is 10 kitta; demat through CDSC; MeroShare for the demat account and IPO applications; TMS for orders through a broker.
- No short selling, futures or options, and no ETFs. Mutual funds are the packaged products. This limits forecasting to a view of long, hold, reduce or avoid.
- Indices: NEPSE, Sensitive, Float, Sensitive Float, and sector sub-indices (commercial banks, development banks, finance, hydropower, hotels and tourism, life insurance, non-life insurance, manufacturing and processing, microfinance, investment, mutual funds, trading, others). Index weights not checked.
- Share types: promoter (with lock-in), public, right, bonus, auction of unsold shares; mergers, suspension, delisting, halts.
- Fiscal year runs Shrawan to Asar (mid-July to mid-July). The FY 2083/84 began 2026-07-17 (from the CGT sources). Quarterly unaudited reports are due within a fixed period after quarter end: window not checked.
- Price tick Rs 0.1: assumed, not checked.

## What the rules mean for analysis

- A 15 percent band makes a locked upper or lower circuit a deeper trap: a seller stuck on a lower lock can face a further 15 percent a day. Always report days to exit at a safe share of daily volume, not just the screen price.
- Volatility and stop distances estimated from data before 2026-04-20 come from a 10 percent regime; do not pool them with later data without saying so. Day-of-week patterns before and after the April 2026 move from Sunday to Friday are different samples.
- Settlement T+2 in trading days: a purchase is not sellable until the shares reach demat, and holidays stretch it.
- Because rules move this fast (price limit, trading days and capital gains tax all changed in 2026), say "as of 2026-10-24, verify" when stating one, and fetch again when the user's question depends on it.
